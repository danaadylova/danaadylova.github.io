{% aside "1-3 end" %}
A love letter to the coast I keep coming back to, and to the slow way of looking that film taught me.

It starts at Timber Cove, where I got married, and wanders south.

<span class="where">Timber Cove → Los Angeles</span>
{% endaside %}

Film makes you wait. You take the picture, forget about it, and months later the lab hands back a day you’d almost lost.

{% aside "1-4 center" %}
Most of these are on 35 mm film, developed and scanned at a lab. The foggy ones from Timber Cove are on my Fujifilm X-T30 II, for the days I can’t wait for the lab.
{% endaside %}

{% aside "2-6 center" %}
California never shows you everything at once. There’s always fog, or a bend in Highway 1, or a cypress in the way. I think that’s why I keep taking pictures of it: to keep a little of what I did get to see.
{% endaside %}
