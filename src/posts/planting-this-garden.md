This site used to just point at [[unraveled-makes]], but I've been wanting a place to put my thoughts, recent projects, and keep a digital journal. I've always been fascinated by blogs, and even though it's not as popular anymore as it was 10/20 years ago, I'd like to give it a go.

So: a digital garden. Everything links to everything with `[[wiki links]]`, like my Obsidian vault.

A few things you might notice:

- Pages link to each other, and each note lists its **backlinks** at the bottom — so you can wander in any direction.
- If you type on your keyboard while you're here, the site thocks like a mechanical keyboard. This serves no purpose, I just like the sound. There's a `sound: off` switch in the footer if you don't.
- It's built with [Eleventy](https://www.11ty.dev/), markdown files, and a GitHub Action. The whole thing is [on GitHub](https://github.com/danaadylova/danaadylova.github.io).
