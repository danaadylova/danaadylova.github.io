## there is an app for that

For years now I have jumped between note-taking apps, trying to keep my projects organized and make sense of the many patterns I've bought. All of them had something good about them, but none fit me fully. I just want to open a PDF on the same page I left it on, <mark>at the exact spot I was at</mark>, with some Ravelry connectivity.

As a note-taking fanatic (yes, I keep 3 journals at any point in my life, and alternate between 3 apps), I also want to <mark>quickly add a note</mark> that saves to Ravelry or stays on my phone.

So I decided to make an app for that. Probably not the most original idea, but so far nothing has been perfect for simply reading patterns. The project is open source and made with AI help. If you happen to try it out, reach out with suggestions on how I can improve it. I'm planning to keep it <mark>free on the App Store</mark>, and if there is interest, bring it to Android later.
